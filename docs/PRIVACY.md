# Text Math privacy policy — publication draft

<!-- Covers the iOS and Android apps. -->

Updated November 1, 2026.

## On your device

Workout settings and the latest 200 rated problem snapshots are stored locally
in the app. Problems are generated on-device; ratings are not sent to an AI
service.

## Sharing

Copy ratings writes your chosen export to the system clipboard. Share ratings
sends the export to the app or destination you select. Those destinations handle
the copy under their own policies.

## Removing data

Ratings can be replaced by rerating a problem, and older ratings are removed
when the 200-rating limit is reached. This version has no clear-all ratings
control. Deleting the app removes its local ratings and settings; copies you
shared and device backups are separate.

On Android, clearing app storage also removes local data. Automatic Android
backup and device transfer are disabled. Save ratings writes a JSON file to the
location chosen in Android's file picker.

Debug builds on both platforms include optional development feedback. Reports
contain a note, current screen, app settings, recent development events and an
optional screenshot. Problem feedback notes and held-value answers may accompany
ratings exports. Choosing a development backup folder enables copying this
feedback to that folder; its provider's policies apply. Release builds omit the
developer feedback controls.

On Android, **Send feedback** in Settings opens your email app with a draft to
`support@plaintxtoffice.com` that includes the app version, Android version and
device model, or opens the contact form on our website. Ratings and problems
are never included, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use feedback to
reply to you, fix bugs and consider feature requests.

## Developer services

This version contains no developer-run analytics, advertising or account
service. Device backups and services you choose, such as sharing destinations or
file providers, follow their own settings and policies.

## Apple diagnostics

We intend to use Apple-provided crash and performance information, when
available under your Apple diagnostic-sharing choices, only to troubleshoot
problems and improve reliability. App diagnostic logging should not
intentionally include your problems, answers, ratings, exported ratings or
other private content. Apple controls its
system-generated reports; we cannot guarantee those reports contain no sensitive
information.

## Questions

Contact opens our website. We use the name, email address and message you
provide to respond to your request. Service providers may process this
correspondence for us. We retain it as needed to resolve the request or meet
legal obligations. Use the same contact form to request deletion.

Contact Plain Text Office: <https://plaintxtoffice.com/contact/>
